Building on the Memorandum of Understanding signed between CityUHK and the Department of Materials Science and Metallurgy at the University of Cambridge, the Lab further strengthened its collaboration with Prof. Jason ROBINSON, Professor of Materials Physics at the University of Cambridge. During this reporting period, Prof. ROBINSON was invited to deliver a seminar entitled 'Supercurrents with a Sense of Direction & Spin: A Spin-Triplet Diode,' with Prof. Yang REN serving as the Chair of the seminar. The seminar introduced local students and young researchers to frontier topics in superconducting spintronics, including spin-triplet supercurrents, superconducting diode effects, non-reciprocal transport, and quantum materials for low-power electronic devices.
