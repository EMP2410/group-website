Dr. Yu PAN from the University of Science and Technology Beijing visited the JC STEM Lab from November 2025 to May 2026 to study the elastic-plastic deformation mechanisms of high-performance titanium alloys. Dr. Pan conducted in-situ tensile tests alongside synchrotron high-energy X-ray experiments for in-depth data analysis on lattice strain evolution and stress-induced phase transformations. This project is expected to deliver fundamental insights for designing superior structural materials and yield high-impact publications.
