Mr. Leung Chi Hon, with a background in Materials Science and Engineering from City University of Hong Kong and internship experience at Imperial College London, has made remarkable progress through nearly one year of full-time research assistantship under the supervision of Prof. Ren Yang. He has developed into a capable early-career researcher. His work spans experimental and computational investigations on energy materials, leading to multiple co-authored publications in international journals such as Chemical Engineering Journal and Small Methods. He will pursue his Ph.D. study in Physics at CityU, under Prof. Ren's supervision.
