The Lab hosted laboratory demonstrations and battery assembly activities as part of a Physics Department summer camp involving approximately 40 local high-school students. Prof. Ren delivered theoretical instruction to the students as part of STEM outreach activities. This outreach helped students build stronger interest in STEM subjects, improved self-confidence, and provided a clearer understanding of how science contributes to society, thereby strengthening Hong Kong's local STEM ecosystem and research capacity at an early stage.
