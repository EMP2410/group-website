On 7 May 2025, the JC STEM Lab hosted Dr. Xin Sun, President of the Overseas Chinese Environmental Engineers and Scientists Association and former Associate Laboratory Director at Oak Ridge National Laboratory (ORNL), for a seminar on 'Challenges and Opportunities in Climate Change and Decarbonization' at City University of Hong Kong. Dr. Sun delivered a comprehensive talk addressing the global CO鈧?induced climate challenge and outlined key decarbonization strategies, including energy efficiency, electrification, low life-cycle carbon fuels, and carbon capture utilization and storage (CCUS). Drawing on her extensive leadership experience in the U.S. Department of Energy, she presented both scientific and policy-relevant insights, supported by case studies and DOE-funded initiatives such as the Carbon Capture Simulation Initiative (CCSI) and CCSI-2.
