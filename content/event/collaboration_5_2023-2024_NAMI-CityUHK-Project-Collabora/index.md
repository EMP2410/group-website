Prof. Ren attended a workshop at the Nano and Advanced Materials Institute Limited (NAMI) to plan project collaborations between CityUHK and NAMI. NAMI is a Hong Kong-based R&D center focused on nano and advanced materials innovation. The workshop provided a platform to explore potential joint research projects, identify areas of mutual interest in materials science and engineering, and discuss mechanisms for technology transfer and commercialization. This collaboration initiative supports the broader goal of connecting CityUHK's synchrotron research capabilities with industrial applications and applied research opportunities.
