Dr. Lihua Liu from the University of Science and Technology Beijing undertook a research attachment from July 2026 to January 2027 focusing on the microstructural investigation of Type-I and Type-VIII Eu8GaxGe46-x systems. By employing synchrotron X-ray diffraction and small-angle neutron scattering, Dr. Liu aimed to accurately characterize the atomic occupancy and disorder distribution within these clathrate compounds. This work provided critical experimental evidence to explain the structural phase transitions and thermoelectric performance of the system.
