During this period, experts from the National Natural Science Foundation of China also visited the JC STEM Lab of Energy and Materials Physics (EMP) for academic exchange. Prof. REN introduced the Lab's research platform, which has been established with the support of The Hong Kong Jockey Club Charities Trust and CityUHK. The visit demonstrated that the Lab has developed a strong foundation for both fundamental and application-oriented research, providing an effective platform for young scholar and student training. This exchange further highlighted the potential of the JC STEM Lab to support future collaboration with NSFC and Mainland research partners.
