Laboratory activity of the JC STEM Lab of Energy and Materials Physics, grouped by the type of
activity. Each entry records the date, venue and a short account of what took place.

- **Conference** — invited talks and scientific meetings attended by the group.
- **Invited Seminar** — seminars given by visiting speakers at CityUHK.
- **Academic Visit** — research visits to partner institutions and to the lab itself.
- **Collaboration** — joint projects and technical exchanges with industrial and academic partners.
- **Student Experiment** — beamtime and hands-on experiments carried out by our students at
  synchrotron and neutron facilities.
- **Training & Visiting Scholar** — visiting scholars hosted by the lab, and the training and
  mentoring of our own students and research talent.
- **Lab Setup & Equipment** — construction, installation and commissioning of the laboratory
  infrastructure and instruments.
