The Lab continues to promote the establishment of a local industry consortium to support synchrotron-enabled research and technology translation. A key engagement was the close collaboration with the Nano and Advanced Materials Institute (NAMI), focusing on advanced manufacturing and scalable development of solid-state lithium-ion batteries. Building on this partnership, the NAMI currently sponsors Mr. WANG Pao Chieh, a local CityUHK graduate, as a part-time Ph.D. student under the supervision of Prof. Yang Ren. Mr. WANG is a member of the research team in NAMI, receiving the award of R&D 100 awards. His dual academic–industry role strengthens talent development while facilitating effective knowledge transfer toward industrial application.
