As part of the Lab's international collaboration activities, Prof. Irina GRIGORIEVA, Professor of Physics at the University of Manchester, was invited by Prof. Yang REN to deliver a seminar entitled 'Unconventional Superconductivity in Materials with Strong Spin–Orbit Coupling,' with Prof. REN serving as the Chair of the seminar. The seminar introduced local students and young researchers to frontier topics in quantum materials, including spin–orbit coupling, unconventional superconductivity, two-dimensional materials, and emergent electronic states. Prof. GRIGORIEVA has close academic links with the University of Manchester's world-leading graphene and two-dimensional materials research platform and is the wife and long-term collaborator of Nobel Laureate Prof. Andre Geim.
