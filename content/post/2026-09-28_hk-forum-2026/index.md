We are pleased to announce the **2026 Hong Kong Forum on Neutron Scattering**, to be held from
**7–11 December 2026** at **Eminence Galaxy, Room 11-231, LAU, City University of Hong Kong**.

<!--more-->

The 2026 edition focuses on **Energy Materials and Applications**, gathering leading experts from
major international neutron scattering facilities together with researchers working on batteries,
fuel cells, hydrogen storage, energy conversion materials and nuclear energy. The forum also gives
students and early-career scientists in Hong Kong and the wider region a chance to learn the
fundamentals and applications of neutron scattering.

- **Dates:** 7–11 December 2026
- **Venue:** Eminence Galaxy, Room 11-231, LAU, City University of Hong Kong
- **Format:** In-person conference, in English
- **Registration:** Free of charge

Invited speakers are being finalised; the confirmed lineup, the scientific programme and the
registration details are listed on the forum page.
