We are pleased to announce the **2026 Hong Kong Forum on Neutron Scattering**, to be held from **8–11 December 2026** at the **Benjamin & Anny Kwok Multi-media Conference Room, City University of Hong Kong**.

The 2026 edition focuses on **Soft Matter and Complex Materials**, gathering leading experts from major international neutron scattering facilities and the soft matter / biophysics community. The forum will also provide opportunities for students and early-career scientists in Hong Kong and the surrounding region to learn the fundamentals and applications of neutron scattering.

<!--more-->

**Key Information**

- **Dates:** 7–11 December 2026 (scientific sessions 8–10 December)
- **Venue:** City University of Hong Kong, Kowloon, Hong Kong
- **Format:** In-person conference
- **Language:** English
- **Registration:** Free of charge

The forum features an invited speaker lineup from **ILL (France), ISIS Neutron and Muon Source (UK), The University of Manchester, KAIST (Korea), J-PARC / CROSS (Japan), University of Tokyo, Peking University, China Spallation Neutron Source (CSNS), UBC (Canada), and CityUHK**.

It is jointly sponsored by the **Croucher Foundation** and the **Center for Neutron Scattering at CityUHK**, funded by UGC / RGC.
