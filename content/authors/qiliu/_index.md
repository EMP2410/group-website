Qi Liu is Professor in the Department of Physics at City University of Hong Kong. He joined CityU in February 2018 as an Assistant Professor, was promoted to Associate Professor in February 2023 and to Professor in February 2026. Before joining CityU, he was a Research Staff member at Argonne National Laboratory, USA (2014–2018), where he worked on neutron scattering instrumentation and its application to energy materials. He obtained his PhD in Physics from Purdue University, USA.

His group at CityU focuses on four directions: fundamental investigation of phase-transformative materials for energy applications, phase-transition behaviour, fundamental studies of the safety of batteries, and the development of multimodal advanced neutron and synchrotron techniques. The ultimate goal is to maximise the efficiency of energy conversion and storage in devices such as batteries, fuel cells and supercapacitors while minimising their environmental impact. His work combines *in situ* neutron and synchrotron X-ray measurements with theory to connect the microscopic structure of cathodes, anodes and oxide catalysts to their macroscopic electrochemical performance, and it has been recognised by a Clarivate Highly Cited Researcher award and by repeated listing in Stanford's World's Top 2% Scientists.
