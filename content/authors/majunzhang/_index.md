Junzhang Ma is Associate Professor in the Department of Physics at City University of Hong Kong. He joined CityU in January 2021 as an Assistant Professor. Before that, he was a joint postdoctoral fellow at the Swiss Light Source, Paul Scherrer Institute (PSI), and École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL) in Switzerland, supported by Prof. Ming Shi and Prof. Joël Mesot. He obtained his PhD from the Institute of Physics (IOP), Chinese Academy of Sciences, under the supervision of Prof. Hong Ding, and his Bachelor's degree from the Department of Physics at Jilin University. His research centers on the electronic structure of quantum materials using Angle-Resolved Photoemission Spectroscopy (ARPES), with a focus on topological materials, unconventional superconductivity, low-dimensional systems, and correlated electron phenomena. He has published over 50 papers in high-impact journals, including *Nature*, *Nature Physics*, *Nature Materials*, *Science Advances* and *Physical Review X*. His work contributed to the discovery of Weyl semimetals, the three-component fermion and the hourglass fermion, and to the observation of mobile excitons in a one-dimensional metal.
