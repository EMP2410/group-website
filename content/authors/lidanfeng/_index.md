Danfeng Li is Associate Professor in the Department of Physics at City University of Hong Kong (CityU), Associate Dean (Research and Postgraduate Education) in the College of Science, and Executive Director of the Hong Kong Institute for Advanced Study (HKIAS). He was promoted to Associate Professor in July 2024. Prior to joining CityU as an Assistant Professor in November 2020, he was a Postdoctoral Research Fellow in the Department of Applied Physics at Stanford University (2017–2020), working with Prof. Harold Y. Hwang, and a Postdoctoral Researcher in the Department of Quantum Matter Physics at the University of Geneva (2016–2017). His research focuses on condensed-matter physics and materials science, particularly atomic-scale fabrication of oxide heterostructures and nanomembranes, kinetic-based synthesis of unconventional quantum materials, low-dimensional superconductivity, and oxide interfaces for emergent states. As a member of the Hwang group at Stanford he contributed to the 2019 discovery of the first nickelate superconductor. He has published in leading journals including *Nature* and *Nature Communications*.
