Yu Chai is Associate Professor in the Department of Physics at City University of Hong Kong. He joined CityU in 2020 as an Assistant Professor after postdoctoral research at Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory (2019–2020) and at the University of California, Berkeley (2016–2019). He obtained his PhD in Physics from the University of Waterloo, Canada, in 2016.

The Chai Research Group asks how soft matter behaves differently at interfaces than in the bulk, and what this can be used for. Their research covers the fundamental questions of soft matter — self-assembly of colloids, surfactants and polymeric systems — while exploring the applications of these materials. Experimental work is carried out with advanced spectroscopic and imaging techniques, supported by the group's own instrument facility at the Yueng Kin Man Academic Building.
