Building on the foundation of the metallic glasses collaboration, the three teams (CityUHK, CityUHK (Dongguan), and industry partner Dongguan Yihao Metal Technology) jointly applied for the Guangdong–Hong Kong Universities '1+1+1' Joint Funding Scheme, with total support of RMB 2 million and HKD 1 million, and the project was successfully funded. This achievement demonstrates how the Lab uses synchrotron and neutron large-scale facilities as a platform to establish domestic collaboration for both education and research activities. It also highlights the effectiveness of cross-border collaboration among CityUHK, CityUHK (Dongguan), and industry partners in promoting joint research, resource sharing, talent development, and technology translation in the Greater Bay Area.
