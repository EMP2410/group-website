To strengthen collaboration within Hong Kong's local R&D community, Prof. Yang REN led Prof. Irina GRIGORIEVA (University of Manchester), together with other professors from the Department of Physics, to visit the Hong Kong Productivity Council (HKPC) for in-depth discussions. The visit aimed to bridge international frontier research with local innovation in Hong Kong, thereby fostering the integration of basic research, advanced characterization techniques, and applied technology development. Discussions explored how the JC STEM Lab's strengths in advanced materials and physical characterization could interface with HKPC's applied R&D and technology translation platforms, creating concrete opportunities for talent development and technology translation.
