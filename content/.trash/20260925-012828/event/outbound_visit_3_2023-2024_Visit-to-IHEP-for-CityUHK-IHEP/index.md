Prof. Ren visited the Institute of High Energy Physics (IHEP), Chinese Academy of Sciences, to initialize an agreement on establishing a CityUHK-IHEP Joint Laboratory for Synchrotron Radiation at the High Energy Photon Source (HEPS) in Beijing. This outbound visit laid the groundwork for a formal institutional collaboration between City University of Hong Kong and IHEP, aiming to leverage the upcoming HEPS facility for advanced synchrotron-based research. The joint laboratory initiative represents a significant step in expanding synchrotron research collaboration between Hong Kong and mainland China.
