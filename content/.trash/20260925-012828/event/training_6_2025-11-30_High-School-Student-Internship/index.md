To strengthen the STEM talent pipeline at an earlier stage, the Lab also engages local high-school students through laboratory internships and outreach activities. During the past semesters, a local high-school student was supervised on projects related to magnetocaloric effects, solid-state cooling, and energy materials. This internship provided early exposure to scientific research, helping to increase curiosity, strengthen foundational knowledge, and positively influence academic aspirations and future study choices.
